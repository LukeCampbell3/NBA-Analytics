headshot-only trading card frontend

Border-Led Refractor Card Lab

A modern sports-card frontend skeleton: the player field stays clean, most parallels affect the border shell, chrome/ice/black finite can affect the full surface, and mojo uses its own interlocking lens geometry instead of generic dots.

Swap headshot with a transparent PNG/SVG headshot path or data URI. The portrait is not boxed; it is layered into the shell like a modern card cutout.

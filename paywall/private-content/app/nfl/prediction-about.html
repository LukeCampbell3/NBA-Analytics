How the NFL Prediction System Works

Architecture

  1. Player form, volume, volatility, and opponent allowances are summarized over lagged three- and five-game windows.
  2. A 16-dimensional encoder predicts the next statistical state from each player’s previous eight games.
  3. Ridge, Extra Trees, XGBoost, CatBoost, and histogram-boosting candidates learn from raw pregame features.
  4. The validated hybrid averages the former per-stat champion with a raw-plus-latent boosted model.
  5. After the untouched holdout is scored, the deployable base models are refit through the validated season.

Backtest Integrity

    What “Accuracy” Means

    • MAE: average absolute difference between predicted and actual yards.
    • R²: share of holdout variance explained by the projection.
    • Within tolerance: the share within 30 passing yards or 15 rushing/receiving yards.
    • Baseline improvement: MAE reduction against a player’s trailing five-game average.

    Market Selection Layer

    1. The market model estimates the probability of finishing over a single posted line; it does not infer betting accuracy from projection MAE.
    2. Regularized raw, raw-plus-latent, CatBoost, and latent CatBoost classifiers are compared by expanding 2021 Brier score.
    3. Passing selected the compact regularized raw-feature classifier. Rushing and receiving failed their independent gates and are suppressed.
    4. A pick needs at least 56% estimated side probability and 2.5 percentage points of no-vig advantage.
    5. An MLB-style ranker keeps at most 12 passing picks per week, with the cap selected only from 2021.

    Production Replay Protocol

    • Every pick is regraded from actual yards, posted line, selected side, and real price.
    • Selection must be deterministic and unchanged when outcome fields are mutated.
    • The pipeline fails closed on schema drift, duplicates, invalid prices, threshold mismatches, wrong architectures, or excess weekly volume.
    • Confidence intervals resample complete NFL weeks to preserve within-week dependence.
    • Half-season, third-season, cap-sensitivity, baseline, and drawdown checks must remain stable.

    Fantasy Draft Model

    1. Position-specific absolute-error models learn only from lagged role, volume, efficiency, volatility, team, and opponent features.
    2. Model depth and global-versus-position blending are selected on 2024; all 2025 weeks remain locked and unseen.
    3. Normalized split-conformal residuals calibrate the central 80% interval and high, medium, and low confidence tiers.
    4. Monte Carlo trials preserve complete correlated stat lines, then center each matchup on the optimized point forecast.
    5. Full-PPR points are ranked by position-adjusted value over replacement, mean production, and simulated upside.

    Validation: The model must pass its 2025 walk-forward gates before the draft payload is written. The frontend displays that result and the holdout metrics directly.

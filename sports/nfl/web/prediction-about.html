How the Yardage Hybrid Works

A predictive player-state encoder blended with compact boosted-tree and regularized tabular models, selected with forward-only NFL seasons.

Architecture

  1. Player form, volume, volatility, and opponent allowances are summarized over lagged three- and five-game windows.
  2. A 16-dimensional encoder predicts the next statistical state from each player’s previous eight games.
  3. Ridge, Extra Trees, XGBoost, CatBoost, and histogram-boosting candidates learn from raw pregame features.
  4. The validated hybrid averages the former per-stat champion with a raw-plus-latent boosted model.
  5. After the untouched holdout is scored, the deployable base models are refit through the validated season.

Backtest Integrity

    What “Accuracy” Means

    • MAE: average absolute difference between predicted and actual yards.
    • R²: share of holdout variance explained by the projection.
    • Within tolerance: the share within 30 passing yards or 15 rushing/receiving yards.
    • Baseline improvement: MAE reduction against a player’s trailing five-game average.
